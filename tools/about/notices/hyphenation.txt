MuPDF 1.28.5 builds the hyphenation patterns of the tex-hyphen project (version CTAN-2025.06.02,
https://github.com/hyphenation/tex-hyphen) into the drawing importer. Each language's patterns have their own authors
and licence (MIT for most; also LPPL, LGPL, MPL and BSD-3-Clause): they are listed, with each copyright notice and
licence text, in resources/hyphen/license/ of MuPDF's source:
https://mupdf.com/downloads/archive/mupdf-1.28.5-source.tar.gz
